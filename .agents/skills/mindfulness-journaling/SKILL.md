# Mindfulness & Reflective Journaling Skill

Facilitates daily reflection, structured evening journaling, emotional check-ins, and stress-reduction break timers.

## Core Routines
1. **Daily Mood & Energy Check-in**: Track morning energy levels and evening reflection on a 1-10 scale in SQLite.
2. **Guided Journaling Prompts**: Provide reflective prompts (e.g. "What was one win today?", "What gave you energy vs drained you?").
3. **Micro-Meditation & Pomodoro Breathing**: Offer 2-minute box breathing guidance (`4s inhale`, `4s hold`, `4s exhale`, `4s hold`) and focus break reminders.
