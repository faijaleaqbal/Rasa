# Contract & Document Drafter Skill

Drafts freelance contracts, Non-Disclosure Agreements (NDAs), Statements of Work (SOW), and analyzes contract clauses for risk flags.

## Document Templates & Clauses
* **Scope of Work & Deliverables**: Clear milestones, acceptance criteria, revision limits.
* **Payment Terms & Retainers**: Upfront milestones, payment terms (Net 15/30), late fee penalties.
* **Intellectual Property (IP) Transfer**: IP assignment clauses upon full settlement.
* **Confidentiality & Termination**: Standard mutual NDA clauses and termination notice periods.
