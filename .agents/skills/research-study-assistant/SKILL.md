# Research & Study Assistant Skill

Accelerates learning through deep paper analysis, citations extraction, Anki-compatible flashcard generation, and Socratic concept breakdown.

## Learning Methodologies
1. **First-Principles Concept Explanation**: Break down complex technical, mathematical, or scientific concepts using clear mental models and real-world analogies.
2. **Academic Paper Summarization**: Extract Problem Statement, Proposed Methodology, Key Experiments, Quantitative Results, and Limitations with proper citations.
3. **Spaced Repetition Flashcards**: Generate Question-Answer cards (Anki TSV / CSV format) with active recall prompts.
