# Client Follow-Up & CRM Automation Skill

Automates outbound follow-up cadences, qualifies inbound leads, synthesizes post-meeting summaries, and manages client relationship timelines.

## Core Workflows
1. **Inbound Lead Qualification (BANT Framework)**: Evaluate Budget, Authority, Need, and Timeline from client inquiries.
2. **Meeting Notes to Action Items**: Process rough call transcripts, extract deliverables, assign deadlines, and generate email recaps.
3. **Automated Follow-Up Cadence**: Send polite, context-aware check-in emails after 3 days, 7 days, and 14 days of proposal delivery.
