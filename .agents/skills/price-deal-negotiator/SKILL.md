# Price Comparison & Deal Negotiator Skill

Audits vendor offerings, compares multi-tier SaaS and e-commerce pricing, finds coupons/deals, and drafts negotiation scripts.

## Core Workflows
1. **Multi-Source Price Matrix**: Compare product specs, shipping fees, return policies, and final checkout prices across Amazon, Flipkart, vendor direct sites.
2. **Feature vs Cost Trade-off**: Create structured comparison tables for SaaS/hosting subscriptions (e.g. AWS vs GCP vs DigitalOcean).
3. **Negotiation Email Drafter**: Generate high-leverage email templates for enterprise discount requests, bulk purchasing, or contract renewals.
